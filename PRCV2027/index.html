大会简介

第十届中国模式识别与计算机视觉学术会议（The 10th Chinese Conference on Pattern Recognition and Computer Vision, PRCV 2027）将于2027年10月15日至17日在甘肃省敦煌市举办。PRCV 是国内模式识别和计算机视觉领域的顶级学术盛会，也是国际上重要且受到国际学术界认可的会议，进入 CCF 分区（CCF-C）。本次会议由中国图象图形学学会（CSIG）、中国人工智能学会（CAAI）、中国计算机学会（CCF）和中国自动化学会（CAA）联合主办，兰州理工大学、敦煌研究院承办。

PRCV 2027 旨在汇聚国内外模式识别与计算机视觉领域的学术和工业界研究人员和专业人士，交流理论研究和技术发展的最新进展。会议还将邀请数十位领域顶尖专家共襄盛会。多位国际讲者、IEEE Fellow 将进行主旨报告和特邀演讲，洞悉领域前沿发展。百余位国家级人才、企业专家参加的十余场专题论坛与讲习班涵盖学术前沿、产业应用与技术创新。女科学家论坛、竞赛论坛、博士生论坛等多元舞台交相辉映，为全球学者搭建展示卓越成果、碰撞思想火花的广阔天地。更有十余家先锋企业共襄会展盛况，深化“产学研”融合，助力模式识别与计算机视觉领域乘风破浪，勇攀高峰。

About the Conference

The 10th Chinese Conference on Pattern Recognition and Computer Vision (PRCV 2027) will take place in Dunhuang, Gansu, China, on October 15–17, 2027. PRCV is a leading Chinese conference in pattern recognition and computer vision. It is recognized by the international research community and is listed in Category C by the China Computer Federation (CCF). The conference is jointly organized by the China Society of Image and Graphics (CSIG), the Chinese Association for Artificial Intelligence (CAAI), the China Computer Federation (CCF), and the Chinese Association of Automation (CAA). Lanzhou University of Technology and Dunhuang Academy will host the meeting.

PRCV 2027 brings together researchers and practitioners from academia and industry in China and abroad to exchange advances in theory and technology. The conference will invite dozens of leading experts. International speakers and IEEE Fellows will give keynote and invited talks on developments at the forefront of the field. More than one hundred nationally recognized researchers and industry experts will take part in over ten thematic forums and tutorials covering research frontiers, industrial applications, and technological innovation. The Women Scientists Forum, Competition Forum, and Doctoral Forum will give scholars from around the world opportunities to present their work and exchange ideas. More than ten pioneering companies will also participate in the exhibition, strengthening collaboration among industry, universities, and research institutes and helping the field reach new heights.
