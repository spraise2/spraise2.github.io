注册费标准

以下收费标准暂定，单位为人民币元。提前注册时段和论文注册缴费截止日期暂定。

注册类别 适用对象 提前注册（元，暂定） 现场注册（元，暂定）
论文注册 录用论文作者 首篇 3,600
第 2、3 篇各 2,880		 暂定
会员注册 具有以下会员身份之一：中国人工智能学会（CAAI）、中国计算机学会（CCF）、中国自动化学会（CAA）或中国图象图形学学会（CSIG）。 2,560 2,800

旅行费用（住宿和交通）不包含在您的注册费用中。

论文注册

每篇录用的论文必须有至少一位论文作者以“论文注册”的方式向会议注册并缴纳会议费，未注册的论文将不能发表。每位作者最多可注册 3 篇论文。

支付方式与退费

以下支付方式暂定，正式收款渠道与注册入口一并公布。请在注册时注明以下信息。

  1. 论文注册：“PRCV2027+您的姓名+论文编号”。
  2. 其他注册类别：“PRCV2027+您的姓名”。
  1. 支付宝付款。点击相应注册类型，填写信息后选择在线支付宝付款，可使用支付宝绑定的公务卡付款。
  2. 微信付款。点击相应注册类型，填写信息后选择在线微信付款，可使用微信绑定的公务卡付款。
  3. 转账付款。正式收款信息公布后，请先办理银行汇款，再在注册时选择银行转账。汇款附言请注明“PRCV2027”和您的姓名，论文注册还需注明论文编号。请在注册完成后上传付款凭证截图。
  4. 现场付款。在会议报到现场填写个人信息后进行注册付款。

按暂定退费规定，10 月 1 日后申请退费或未参加会议的，不予退款。退费办法以正式注册通知为准。

邀请函与发票

会议邀请函 PDF 下载入口暂定。发票开具及发送时间、注册缴费与发票咨询邮箱暂定。

如您需要换开发票，请按发票换开模板填写信息，在右下角加上手写电子签名，发送至指定邮箱。邮件标题请注明“PRCV2027发票换开申请”。模板和接收邮箱待公布。

现场报到

大会会期为 2027 年 10 月 15–17 日，地点为甘肃敦煌。现场报到日期、地点及办理安排暂定。

Registration fees

The fees below are provisional and are given in Chinese yuan (CNY). The advance registration period and the payment deadline for author registration are to be confirmed.

Registration category Eligibility Advance fee (CNY, provisional) On-site fee (CNY, provisional)
Author registration Authors of accepted papers First paper 3,600
Second and third papers 2,880 each		 To be confirmed
Member registration Members of the Chinese Association for Artificial Intelligence (CAAI), the China Computer Federation (CCF), the Chinese Association of Automation (CAA), or the China Society of Image and Graphics (CSIG). 2,560 2,800
Student registration Full-time students must provide a scan of their student ID. In-service postgraduate students and postdoctoral researchers are ineligible for student registration. 1,500 1,800
Full registration Open to all participants 3,200 3,500

Registration fees do not cover accommodation or transportation.

Author registration

For each accepted paper, at least one author must complete author registration and pay the conference fee. A paper cannot be published without registration. Each author may register up to three papers.

Payment and refunds

The following payment methods are provisional. Payment channels will be announced when registration opens. Please include the following details in the payment reference.

  1. Author registration: “PRCV2027+Your Name+Paper ID”.
  2. All other registration categories: “PRCV2027+Your Name”.
  1. Alipay. Select your registration category, enter your details, and choose Alipay. You may use an official business credit card linked to Alipay.
  2. WeChat Pay. Select your registration category, enter your details, and choose WeChat Pay. You may use an official business credit card linked to WeChat Pay.
  3. Bank transfer. Once the bank details are published, make your transfer and then select bank transfer when you register. Include “PRCV2027” and your name in the transfer reference. Authors must also include their paper ID. Upload a screenshot of the payment receipt after registration.
  4. On-site payment. Complete your registration details and pay at the conference check-in desk.

Under the provisional refund policy, refunds will not be issued for requests made after 1 October 2027 or for non-attendance. The final terms will be published in the registration notice.

Invitation letters and invoices

The PDF invitation letter download link is to be confirmed. The invoice issue and delivery dates and the contact email for registration payments and invoices are also to be confirmed.

To request a replacement invoice, complete the invoice replacement form, add your handwritten electronic signature in the lower right corner, and email it to the designated address. Use the subject line “PRCV2027发票换开申请” (PRCV 2027 replacement invoice request). The form and email address will be announced.

Conference check-in

The conference takes place from 15 to 17 October 2027 in Dunhuang, Gansu. The check-in date, location, and arrangements are to be confirmed.
