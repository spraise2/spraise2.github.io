讲习班征集

现诚挚邀请广大模式识别与计算机视觉领域的从业者在 PRCV 2027 会议上组织或承办各种讲习班活动。讲习班针对计算机视觉、模式识别与机器学习等领域的某一特定方向，由若干名讲者从不同维度、不同视角或不同侧面，对该方向的理论、方法与应用进行详细讲解。

有意愿在 PRCV 2027 组织或承办讲习班活动者，请于 2027 年 8 月 30 日前提交 PRCV 2027 讲习班申请，以电子邮件形式发送给讲习班主席，并抄送大会邮箱。申请结果将于 2027 年 9 月 10 日公布。所有被采纳的讲习班信息将同步发布在 PRCV 2027 官方网站。

讲习班申请材料

讲习班申请需包括以下信息。

  • 讲习班主题。
  • 讲习班组织者的姓名、所属机构以及联系信息。
  • 讲习班组织者的简历。
  • 讲习班的主题介绍、目的及意义。
  • 若讲习班计划超过半天，请给出申请额外活动时间的理由。

提交方式

请有意组织讲习班的同行填写好讲习班申请书（PDF 格式），以邮件形式同时发送至下列四位讲习班主席，并抄送大会邮箱。申请模板待更新，大会抄送邮箱待确认。

Call for Tutorial Proposals

Researchers and practitioners in pattern recognition and computer vision are warmly invited to organize or host tutorials at PRCV 2027. Each tutorial focuses on a particular topic in computer vision, pattern recognition or machine learning, with several speakers exploring its theory, methods and applications from different perspectives.

To propose a tutorial, email the tutorial chairs by 30 August 2027 and copy the conference email address. Decisions will be announced on 10 September 2027. Details of all accepted tutorials will be published on the official PRCV 2027 website.

Proposal requirements

Please include the following information in your proposal.

  • Tutorial topic.
  • Names, affiliations and contact details of the organizers.
  • Organizers’ CVs.
  • A description of the topic, its objectives and significance.
  • If the tutorial is expected to last more than half a day, explain why additional time is needed.

How to submit

Please email the completed tutorial proposal in PDF format to all four tutorial chairs listed below, copying the conference email address. The application template and conference email address will be announced here.
