现诚挚邀请广大模式识别与计算机视觉领域的从业者在 PRCV 2027 会议上组织或承办各种专题论坛活动。专题论坛针对计算机视觉、模式识别与机器学习等领域的某一特定方向，由若干名讲者从不同维度、不同视角或不同侧面，对该方向的理论、方法与应用进行详细讲解。

有意愿在 PRCV 2027 组织或承办专题论坛活动者，请于 2027 年 8 月 30 日前提交 PRCV 2027 专题论坛申请，以电子邮件形式发送给专题论坛主席，并抄送大会邮箱。申请结果将于 2027 年 9 月 10 日公布。所有被采纳的专题论坛信息将同步发布在 PRCV 2027 官方网站。

专题论坛申请材料

专题论坛申请需包括以下信息。

  • 专题论坛提案的标题。
  • 专题论坛组织者的姓名、所属机构以及联系信息。
  • 专题论坛组织者的简历。
  • 专题论坛的主题介绍、初步日程安排、拟邀请讲者的个人简介及报告题目。
  • 若专题论坛计划超过半天，请给出申请额外活动时间的理由。

提交方式

请有意组织专题论坛的同行填写好专题论坛申请书（PDF 格式），以邮件形式同时发送至下列四位专题论坛主席，并抄送大会邮箱。大会抄送邮箱待确认。

Researchers and practitioners in pattern recognition and computer vision are warmly invited to organize or host thematic forums at PRCV 2027. Each thematic forum focuses on a particular topic in computer vision, pattern recognition or machine learning, with several speakers exploring its theory, methods and applications from different perspectives.

To propose a thematic forum, email the forum chairs by 30 August 2027 and copy the conference email address. Decisions will be announced on 10 September 2027. Details of all accepted thematic forums will be published on the official PRCV 2027 website.

Proposal requirements

Please include the following information in your proposal.

  • Title of the forum proposal.
  • Names, affiliations and contact details of the organizers.
  • Organizers’ CVs.
  • A description of the topic, a preliminary schedule, and short biographies and talk titles for the proposed speakers.
  • If the forum is expected to last more than half a day, explain why additional time is needed.

How to submit

Please email the completed forum proposal in PDF format to all four forum chairs listed below, copying the conference email address. The conference email address will be announced here.
