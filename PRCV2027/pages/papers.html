第十届中国模式识别与计算机视觉学术会议（The 10th Chinese Conference on Pattern Recognition and Computer Vision，PRCV 2027）将于 2027 年 10 月 15 日至 17 日在甘肃敦煌举办。PRCV 是国内模式识别和计算机视觉领域的顶级学术盛会，也受到国际学术界认可，进入 CCF 分区（CCF-C）。本次会议由中国图象图形学学会（CSIG）、中国人工智能学会（CAAI）、中国计算机学会（CCF）和中国自动化学会（CAA）联合主办，兰州理工大学、敦煌研究院承办。

PRCV 2027 旨在汇聚国内外模式识别与计算机视觉领域的学术和工业界研究人员和专业人士，交流理论研究和技术发展的最新进展。会议还将邀请数十位领域顶尖专家共襄盛会。多位国内外院士、IEEE Fellow 将进行主旨报告和特邀演讲，洞悉领域前沿发展。百余位国家级人才、企业专家参加的十余场专题论坛与讲习班涵盖学术前沿、产业应用与技术创新。女科学家论坛、竞赛论坛、博士生论坛等多元舞台交相辉映，为全球学者搭建展示卓越成果、碰撞思想火花的广阔天地。更有十余家先锋企业共襄会展盛况，深化“产学研”融合，助力模式识别与计算机视觉领域乘风破浪，勇攀高峰。

我们诚挚邀请广大科技工作者投递高质量、原创性的优秀英文学术论文。录用的论文将在大会上交流，会议论文集将由 Springer 出版社出版，并被 EI 和 ISTP 检索。您的参与不仅将为大会增添光彩，也将助力我国模式识别与计算机视觉领域迈向更高水平！

论文初稿提交说明

  • 论文必须使用英文，且之前未曾发表过。
  • 论文不得超过 14 页（包括参考文献），须以 PDF 格式提交，并附上补充材料（如适用）。
  • 论文必须匿名提交，作者的姓名和所属机构不得出现在 PDF 或补充材料中。如果文本包含可识别作者身份的信息，则必须将其隐去。非匿名的论文将被拒绝。
  • 请确保 CMT 系统中（而非论文本身）的所有作者姓名均采用“名字 姓氏”格式，例如“Xiaoming Li”，而不是“Li Xiaoming”或“Li, Xiaoming”。
  • 论文被接受后，不允许对作者名单进行任何更改，包括更改作者顺序或添加、删除任何作者。

论文最终版提交说明

终稿提交日期为 2027 年 8 月 10 日，Camera Ready 提交网址暂定。终稿不要超过 15 页。请提交一个独立的 ZIP 压缩文件，命名为 camera_ready_paper_id_xxxx.zip，其中 xxxx 为论文编号。压缩包需要包含以下材料。

  1. copyright.pdf 文件。请在 copyright.docx 中填写论文题目及作者信息，手写签名后扫描为 PDF。本届版权表下载链接暂定。
  2. original_materials 文件夹。包含原始 TeX 文件（确保能够编译成功、无文件缺失）或 Word 源文件，以及相关图片。
  3. paper_id_xxxx.pdf 文件。此文件为最终版论文 PDF。

学术伦理重要提醒

重大错误处理

一旦发现已发表作品中存在重大错误或失实陈述，作者有义务进行修正。文献更正方式将根据错误性质决定，可能为勘误或撤稿，出版方将根据出版伦理委员会（COPE）指南作出决定。

学术伦理违规处理

若存在学术不端行为或涉嫌学术欺诈，出版方将依据 COPE 指南展开调查。经查证若存在实质性违规，出版方将通过作者登记邮箱联系涉事作者，给予申述机会。根据违规性质及严重程度，出版方可能采取包括但不限于以下措施。

  • 若书稿提案或初稿仍在评审阶段，将会被退回并返还给作者。
  • 若该工作已在线发表，依据违规行为的性质与严重程度，可能在作品的在线版本附加勘误声明，并同步至印刷版（精装本、MyCopy、平装本）。情节严重时可能会撤稿。
  • 向作者所属机构通报违规行为。
  • 在作者及作品文献记录中标注涉嫌违反学术伦理的行为。

须在发布的勘误声明或撤稿声明中详细说明原因。作品的电子版本将继续保留在出版平台，但会添加“已撤稿”水印标识，同时通过注释链接提供完整的撤稿说明文件。已印刷的纸质版本将停止发行并下架。根据学术不端行为的严重程度，出版方也可选择彻底移除作品电子版本，仅保留元数据中的撤稿说明链接以供追溯核查。

The 10th Chinese Conference on Pattern Recognition and Computer Vision (PRCV 2027) will take place in Dunhuang, Gansu, China, from 15 to 17 October 2027. PRCV is a leading Chinese conference in pattern recognition and computer vision. It is recognized by the international research community and is listed in Category C by the China Computer Federation (CCF). The conference is jointly organized by the China Society of Image and Graphics (CSIG), the Chinese Association for Artificial Intelligence (CAAI), the China Computer Federation (CCF) and the Chinese Association of Automation (CAA). Lanzhou University of Technology and the Dunhuang Academy are the local hosts.

PRCV 2027 brings together researchers and practitioners from academia and industry in China and around the world to share recent advances in the theory and technology of pattern recognition and computer vision. The conference will invite dozens of leading experts. Members of Chinese and international academies and IEEE Fellows will present keynote and invited talks on emerging research. More than 100 nationally recognized researchers and industry experts will take part in over ten forums and tutorials on research frontiers, industrial applications and technological innovation. The Women Scientists Forum, competition forums and the Doctoral Forum will offer researchers from around the world opportunities to present their work and exchange ideas. More than ten pioneering companies will participate in the exhibition, strengthening collaboration among industry, universities and research institutes and advancing the field.

We warmly invite researchers to submit high-quality, original papers in English. Accepted papers will be presented at the conference. The proceedings will be published by Springer and indexed by EI and ISTP. Your participation will enrich the conference and help advance pattern recognition and computer vision in China.

Springer LNCS author guidelines and templates

Initial submission instructions

  • Papers must be written in English and must not have been published previously.
  • Submit the paper as a PDF of no more than 14 pages, including references. Include any supplementary material, if applicable.
  • Submissions must be anonymous. Authors’ names and affiliations must not appear in the PDF or in supplementary material. Remove or anonymize any other information that identifies the authors. Non-anonymous submissions will be rejected.
  • Enter all authors’ names in CMT in given-name–family-name order, for example “Xiaoming Li”. Forms such as “Li Xiaoming” or “Li, Xiaoming” are unsuitable. This instruction applies to CMT records. The paper itself must remain anonymous.
  • The author list is final once a paper is accepted. Authors may not be added, removed or reordered.

Camera-ready submission instructions

Camera-ready papers are due on 10 August 2027. The submission link will be announced here. The final paper must not exceed 15 pages. Submit a single ZIP archive named camera_ready_paper_id_xxxx.zip, replacing xxxx with your paper ID. Include all of the following.

  1. copyright.pdf. Enter the paper title and author information in copyright.docx, sign it by hand, and scan the signed form as a PDF. The copyright form for this edition will be made available here.
  2. An original_materials folder containing the TeX source (including every file needed to compile it) or the Word source file, along with the figures.
  3. paper_id_xxxx.pdf, the final camera-ready paper in PDF format.

Publication ethics

Significant errors

If authors discover a significant error or misstatement in published work, they have a duty to correct it. Depending on the nature of the error, the publisher will determine whether to issue a correction or a retraction in accordance with the guidelines of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE).

Breaches of publication ethics

The publisher will investigate misconduct or suspected academic fraud in accordance with COPE guidelines. If substantial grounds for a breach are found, the authors concerned will be contacted at their registered email addresses and given an opportunity to respond. Depending on the nature and severity of the breach, the publisher may take the following or other measures.

  • A proposal or manuscript still under review may be rejected and returned to its authors.
  • For work already published online, a correction may be added to the online version and to the printed versions (hardcover, MyCopy and paperback). Serious breaches may lead to retraction.
  • The authors’ institutions may be informed of the breach.
  • A note of the suspected breach may be added to the records of the authors and the work.

Correction and retraction notices must explain the reasons in detail. When a work is retracted, its electronic version will normally remain on the publishing platform with a “Retracted” watermark and a link to the full retraction notice. Printed copies will be withdrawn from sale and distribution. Depending on the severity of the misconduct, the publisher may instead remove the electronic version entirely and retain a link to the retraction notice in its metadata for verification.
