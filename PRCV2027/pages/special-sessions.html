特别专题（Special Session）是 PRCV 会议的重要组成部分，旨在围绕模式识别和计算机视觉领域中的新兴方向和交叉议题，组织具有针对性的小规模专题研讨。每个特别专题将聚焦一个具体主题，涵盖该方向中具有代表性的最新研究成果，以促进特定领域的深入交流和合作。

评审与出版

特别专题接受定向邀稿与公开投稿。论文须通过 PRCV 2027 官方投稿系统，选择对应专题会议选项提交，与主会议投稿采用相同评审标准和程序。被接收的特别专题论文将在大会上交流，统一收录于 PRCV 2027 正式论文集。会议论文集将由 Springer 出版社出版，并被 EI 和 ISTP 检索。

特别专题投稿时间

事项 日期
投稿截止时间 2027 年 7 月 10 日 23:59（UTC+8）
录用通知日期 2027 年 8 月 1 日（与主会议一致）
终稿提交日期 2027 年 8 月 10 日（与主会议一致）

投稿步骤

  1. 登录 PRCV 2027 投稿系统，投稿网址暂定。
  2. 点击左上角的“Create new submission”，选择“Special Sessions”。
  3. 在“Subject Areas”中选择意向投稿的特别专题，其他论文信息的填写要求与主会议投稿要求相同。

Special sessions are an integral part of PRCV. These focused, small-scale sessions cover emerging directions and interdisciplinary questions in pattern recognition and computer vision. Each session addresses a specific topic and presents representative recent work to encourage in-depth discussion and collaboration.

Review and publication

Special sessions accept both invited papers and open submissions. Authors must submit through the official PRCV 2027 submission system and select the relevant session. The review criteria and procedures are the same as those for the main conference. Accepted special session papers will be presented at the conference and included in the official PRCV 2027 proceedings, which will be published by Springer and indexed by EI and ISTP.

Special session dates

Milestone Date
Submission deadline 10 July 2027, 23:59 (UTC+8)
Notification of acceptance 1 August 2027 (same as the main conference)
Camera-ready paper deadline 10 August 2027 (same as the main conference)

How to submit

  1. Sign in to the PRCV 2027 submission system. The link will be announced here.
  2. Select “Create new submission” at the upper left and choose “Special Sessions”.
  3. Under “Subject Areas”, select the intended special session. Complete the remaining fields as described in the main conference submission instructions.
