PRCV 2027 设钻石、铂金、金牌、银牌和铜牌五个不同级别的合作单位，包含通用权益和各级别权益。如有特殊需求，请与组委会联系。

合作单位共同权益

  1. 在大会网站、会议手册和背景板上展示企业标识。
  2. 在大会晚宴颁奖典礼上，接受大会颁发的致谢奖牌。
  3. 在会议资料包中发放企业介绍资料或小礼品。
  4. 在大会网站上发布一次招聘信息。

权益安排

各项如涉及合作单位顺序，均按钻石、铂金、金牌、银牌、铜牌单位顺序排列。同一级别的合作单位按实际到款顺序排列。

论坛演讲以赞助款到账时间排序。请提前三周与赞助主席沟通演讲主题和讲者人选。

Sponsorship

On this page
  • Benefits for all sponsors
  • Benefits by sponsorship level
  • Sponsorship arrangements
  • Sponsorship enquiries

PRCV 2027 offers Diamond, Platinum, Gold, Silver, and Bronze sponsorships. Each level includes shared benefits and benefits specific to that level. Please contact the Organizing Committee about special requirements.

Benefits for all sponsors

  1. Display of the company logo on the conference website, in the conference handbook, and on the conference backdrop.
  2. A plaque of appreciation presented at the conference banquet awards ceremony.
  3. Distribution of company brochures or small gifts in the conference delegate packs.
  4. One recruitment posting on the conference website.

Sponsorship arrangements

Where sponsors are listed in order, the levels appear as Diamond, Platinum, Gold, Silver, and Bronze. Sponsors within the same level are listed in the order in which payments are received.

Forum presentations are scheduled in the order in which sponsorship payments are received. Please discuss the topic and choice of speaker with the sponsorship chairs three weeks in advance.

Sponsorship enquiries

For sponsorship enquiries or special requirements, please contact the sponsorship chairs. Contact details are to be confirmed.
